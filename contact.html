FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I book a viewing?

Use any "Book a viewing" button on the site, call us, or send a message above. We offer in-person viewings seven days a week and live video tours for overseas buyers.

Do you charge buyers a fee?

No. Our fees are paid by the seller or landlord. If you appoint us as a dedicated buying agent for an off-market search, we'll agree a transparent fee upfront.

How is my home valued?

A senior partner visits in person and combines recent comparable sales, live demand data from our buyer register and a detailed assessment of your home's condition and features.

Can international buyers purchase in The Halcyon Tower?

Yes. We regularly assist overseas buyers and can introduce trusted legal, tax and finance partners to guide you through the process remotely.

What's included in your property management service?

Tenant sourcing and referencing, rent collection, maintenance coordination, inspections, compliance and monthly owner reporting through our online portal.
