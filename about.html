Our story

Founded on a simple idea: treat every home like our own.

Halcyon began in 2004 as a two-person office above a bookshop in Old Town. Today our team of 42 advisors represents buyers, sellers, landlords and developers across the city — and still answers the phone personally.

We are proud to be the exclusive sales partner for The Halcyon Tower, the city's tallest residential address, and to have placed more than 1,350 families in homes they love.
